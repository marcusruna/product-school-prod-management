# Feature Roadmap, Module 4 · StreamLine Spotlight

**Team:** 2 engineers + 1 designer

## Strategic anchors
- **Persona:** The Overwhelmed Veteran: a long-tenured subscriber who once used StreamLine as her default evening entertainment but now behaves like a Wanderer
- **Primary metric:** Share of Wanderer sessions reaching 30+ minutes after engaging with Spotlight. It’s a leading indicator that moves before churn does. Month 1 retention is the lagging confirmation.
- **Moment of misery:** She opens the app, scrolls for about twenty minutes, and closes it without watching anything. She ends up putting on a DVD instead. The catalog’s size, which should be a reason to stay, has become a reason to give up. Marcus adds the trust dimension: the recommendations treat him as a genre (“I’m not a genre, I’m a person”), so he has stopped believing the app can surface anything good.
- **Guardrail:** Power User sessions/week (currently 4.8×) and churn. Power Users already watch 58% curated content, and Spotlight must not cannibalize or disrupt the segment that already works.

## Roadmap
### NOW, 3-week sprint
- **A1 Spotlight Curated Rail**, It replaces the twenty-minute scroll with a short, human-picked set and answers Marcus’s “I’m not a genre” complaint by taking the algorithm out of the loop.
- **A2 'Why You'll Love This' Label**, Descoped to curator-written one-liners on rail titles, which fit the trust problem better than AI reasons and work on TV, where there’s no hover.

### NEXT, following 1-2 sprints
- **A3 Hidden Gem Badge**, It’s cheap, but it adds one more signal to weigh instead of fewer choices; applied only inside the rail.
- **A6 Spotlight Digest Email**, M3 retention data shows [lapsing Wanderers still engage with email]; it puts a decision in front of her before she opens the app, with picks pulled from the rail.

### LATER, backlog
- **A4 Mood-Based Entry Point**, It needs a mood taxonomy across the whole catalog, and a login gate puts friction on Power Users, which risks the guardrail.
- **A5 Personalized Spotlight Queue**, M3 cohort data shows [list-savers reach 30+ min sessions more often], but Effort 4 doesn’t fit this sprint; revisit once A1 proves curation rebuilds trust.
- **A7 Curator Profiles**, Human taste is the right answer to the trust problem, but follow mechanics are scope creep before A1 is proven; for now, just put the curator’s name and face on the rail.

### ✂ Cut List
- **A8 Watch Party (Spotlight)**, It solves a social problem no M2 interviewee raised; the request comes from Sales, not from her friction.
- **A9 Advanced Filter Engine**, More filters add to the choice overload behind the Moment of Misery and mostly serve Power Users, who already succeed.
- **A10 Offline Download (Spotlight)**, DRM and licensing take months, and nothing in the Moment of Misery involves connectivity.
